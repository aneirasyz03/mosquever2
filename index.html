THE STORY

A Timeline of Seoul Central Mosque

1950s

Islam grows after the Korean War

Islam began developing again in Korea after the Korean War, with Turkish military personnel playing a role in early outreach. The Korean Muslim community began to take shape, later developing into the Korean Muslim Federation (KMF).

1969

Land grant in Itaewon

A 5,000 m² site in Itaewon was granted to support the growing Muslim community and international relations.

1974

Construction begins

Construction of the mosque began in October 1974, supported by contributions from Muslim countries, with major support reported from Saudi Arabia.

21 May 1976

Seoul Central Mosque opens

The mosque was officially opened for public use and became an important center for Muslim life, education, and community activities in Korea.

1990

Expansion

The original two-storey building was expanded to three floors, adding educational and community facilities.
